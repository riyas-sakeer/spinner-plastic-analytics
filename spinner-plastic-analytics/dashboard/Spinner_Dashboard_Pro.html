Key Insights

  • Revenue grew consistently at 5% YoY from ₹321M (2022) to ₹370M (2025)
  • uPVC and HDPE Pipes drive 47% of total revenue combined

  • Gross margin stable at ~18.57% across all four financial years
  • Term loan reduced from ₹5 Cr to ₹3.5 Cr — healthy debt trajectory
  • Trivandrum and Kollam have zero sales — major growth opportunity

Margin Insights

  • Manufacturing products cost 85.5% of sales (RM: 67.5% + Overhead: 18%), leaving 14.5% gross margin
  • Trading products cost 77.5% of sales (Purchase: 70% + Expenses: 7.5%), earning higher 22.5% margin
  • Despite higher margin %, trading contributes only 5% of revenue — limiting absolute profit contribution
  • Margins are stable across all 4 years — reflects controlled cost structure and consistent pricing

Financial Highlights

  • Sales grew from ₹35.0 Cr (FY2020) to ₹40.5 Cr (FY2023) at 5% annually
  • Gross margin stable at ~18.57% — consistent cost management across all years
  • Net margin stable at ~5.0% — modest but steady bottom-line profitability
  • Purchases (raw materials) are the dominant cost at 77–79% of sales revenue
  • Depreciation fixed at ₹1 Cr per year — no accelerated asset write-down
  • Bank charges and interest at ₹1.2–1.4 Cr reflect continued loan dependency
